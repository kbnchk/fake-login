What we collect

We process the account details you provide (name, work email) and technical data needed to run the service, such as IP address, browser type and sign-in timestamps.

How we use it

  • To authenticate you and keep your workspace secure.
  • To maintain, monitor and improve the service.
  • To meet legal and contractual obligations.

Retention

Sign-in logs are kept for up to 90 days. Account data is deleted within 30 days after a workspace is closed.
